Research

I study how citizens judge politicians, protesters and the far right, mostly through survey experiments run across Europe.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Bergen, working with Ragnhild Muriaas on the SUCCESS project. Before that I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Humboldt University of Berlin on the COVIDEU project, and I received my PhD in Political Science from Trinity College Dublin. My work sits in political behavior and comparative politics and relies on experimental methods and causal inference.

  • Gender and political representation
  • Support for the far right
  • Protest and protest rights
  • Legitimacy in hybrid regimes

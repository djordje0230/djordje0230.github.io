Welcome!

I am Đorđe Milosav, currently a postdoctoral researcher at the SUCCESS project at the University of Bergen. Previosly, I held a postdoctoral position in Political Behavior at Humboldt University of Berlin, as a part of the COVIDEU project. I received my PhD in Political Science from Trinity College Dublin. My current work is broadly situated within the fields of political behavior and comparative politics, relies on experimental methods and causal inference and focuses geographically on Europe.
